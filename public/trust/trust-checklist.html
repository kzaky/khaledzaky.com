The Trust Checklist

Four questions to ask before any AI output reaches a real decision. Ask them about your own work first.
1. Can every fact name its source?Not "I researched it." A source. If a number cannot say where it came from, it is an assumption wearing a suit.
2. Does something automatically check that source, or just a human on a good day?Humans skim. Experts skim faster. The check has to be a thing that runs, not a thing you intend to do.
3. When the check fails, does it block, or does it warn and get ignored?Warnings are ignored by the third week. A failed check should stop the document from shipping until a human decides.
4. Can you show someone the receipt six months from now?Every claim, its source, whether it passed. When the VP asks where 62% came from, this is the only good answer.
If any answer is no, you have a demo. Not yet a teammate. That is fine. Now you know which of the four to fix first.
