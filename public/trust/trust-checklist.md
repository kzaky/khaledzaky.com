# The Trust Checklist
Four questions to ask before any AI output reaches a real decision.

1. **Can every fact name its source?** If a number cannot say where it came from, it is an assumption wearing a suit.
2. **Does something automatically check that source, or just a human on a good day?** The check has to be a thing that runs, not a thing you intend to do.
3. **When the check fails, does it block, or does it warn and get ignored?** Warnings are ignored by the third week. A failed check stops the document until a human decides.
4. **Can you show someone the receipt six months from now?** Every claim, its source, whether it passed.

If any answer is no, you have a demo. Not yet a teammate.

Engineers call this an eval. Risk calls it a guardrail. Regulators call it governance. Same four questions.
